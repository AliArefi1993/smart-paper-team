# Smart Paper growth and community

Read `STATUS.md` and [the onboarding brief](../../docs/growth-community.md), then only the relevant product, release, feedback, or task evidence. This repository-local skill is loaded explicitly by the Growth agent; do not assume global installation. Keep facts in their authoritative documents instead of copying release details here.

## Prepare a bounded experiment

Use an assigned task under `tasks/` for meaningful work. Record the prospective audience and evidence, their job or friction, one supported value proposition, chosen channel and why it fits, material to review, install path, effort/budget limit, observation window, and a decision rule for continuing or changing direction. Mark untested audience and channel assumptions `Needs confirmation`; do not imply product-market fit.

Research current external channel rules, distribution requirements, and recommendations when needed; cite sources and dates. Do not pick platforms from stale assumptions. Missing audience or budget information need not prevent drafting options, but is not permission to recruit or spend.

For public materials, check claims against `PRODUCT.md` and the current matching release record. Prepare concise copy in the requested language; label unreviewed translations. Use real screenshots with synthetic/sample data and ask Designer for visual review through the lead. Never use personal device data, fabricated testimonials, or claims of guaranteed productivity, encryption, cloud sync, store listing, or device validation without evidence. Installation guidance must identify the official signed artifact and explain the actual distribution path without encouraging users to disable security protections broadly.

## Learn from use

Define a first useful action suited to the proposed audience (for example, saving a day plan). Keep invited/reached people, downloads, self-confirmed installs, that action, and voluntary return over a stated window distinct. State counts, denominators, source, and missing observations; unknown is not zero. Use a small consented manual pilot initially, not new telemetry. Do not ask participants for private plans, financial records, Idea content, credentials, or diagnostic exports to prove adoption.

Store only sanitized aggregate findings and non-identifying themes in Git; keep private contacts and reports outside the repository. Route defects through [feedback triage](../../docs/feedback-triage.md), value/scope findings to Product, and installation/interaction friction to Product and Designer. Distinguish audience mismatch, distribution friction, and product friction before proposing more features.

## Execution boundaries

For @smart_paper_app, the maintainer authorized Growth to handle the channel on 2026-10-16 with Persian-only prose. Read [the channel handoff](../../docs/growth/telegram-channel.md) for scope, publishing commands and public receipts. Routine verified-release shares, summaries and useful app tips within this scope do not need repeated per-post permission. This is channel publication authorization, not permission to create a new Android release, contact people privately, spend, or schedule unattended work. Keep URLs and original artifact identifiers intact while writing prose in Persian.

Deliver concrete materials before requesting any missing publication authorization. External posting, messages, recruitment, spending, account changes, and schedules need explicit user authorization for that action; role setup or a draft request alone does not grant it. Follow existing authorization when present. Stop at an unapproved external action while completing independent preparation.

Product owns priorities, Designer owns design, and the lead owns launch/release go/no-go. App changes follow the existing engineering/design workflow. Keep physical-device gaps visible without inventing new release gates, and flag the existing pre-user release-policy review when actual users begin. Close each experiment with evidence, limitations, and one recommended next step.
