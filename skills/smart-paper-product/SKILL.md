# Smart Paper product brief

Start with `STATUS.md`, then read only the relevant parts of `PRODUCT.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, existing `design/` records, task files, and implementation. The Android local-data app is primary (decision D-007).

For a meaningful user-facing change, write or update a brief using `design/TEMPLATE.md`. Explain the user's job, current friction, evidence, desired outcome, current product behavior, constraints, acceptance criteria, and non-goals. Separate observed facts, user direction, and hypotheses. Label unsupported assumptions `Needs confirmation`; do not invent research findings or product capabilities.

Give Designer a problem and constraints without dictating a screen. Define success with observable behavior, including English/Persian use and data safety when relevant. If options differ materially, state their tradeoffs and recommend one. Keep the brief concise enough to guide one improvement cycle.
