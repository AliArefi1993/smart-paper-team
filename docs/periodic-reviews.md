## Problem, Evidence And Outcome

Per-change checks cover agreed work well, but can miss interactions across routes and accumulating maintenance needs. [STATUS](../STATUS.md) identifies remaining native Android, upgrade/restore, TalkBack and broad integration gaps. The [accepted CI/local-test policy](ci-local-test-policy.md) distinguishes deterministic/desktop evidence from device evidence and permits exact-commit reuse. The [batching decision](../tasks/2026-10-08-batching-release-cadence.md) preserves the current publication trigger. These facts support one bounded shared checkpoint; they do not establish an optimal frequency or guaranteed savings.

Desired outcome: recognize significant regressions and evidence-backed improvements before they accumulate, with a small review cost. Android offline/local-data remains primary; backend/hosted-web feature work stays paused. No telemetry, analytics system, new project-management tool or mandatory separate chat is proposed. Design is not applicable to this policy; future user-visible work still requires a ready bilingual Designer handoff.

## Cadence Options And Adopted Decision

| Option | User value | Risk / overhead | Implementation size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Check every release | Frequent attention | Repeats evidence during rapid small releases; adds a gate | Small policy, high recurring effort |
| Calendar schedule/automation | Predictable reminder | Runs without useful change/context; requires separate authorization and upkeep | Larger setup and recurring effort |
| Manual checkpoint at a natural work boundary, with count/time cues and risk triggers | Combines cross-app confidence and discovery when context is already available | May be delayed between sessions; maintainer must accept that tradeoff | Small documentation-only setup |

The maintainer adopted the third option on 2026-10-13. Run the first manual baseline checkpoint at the next normal session or completed batch boundary. Counts start at adoption; after the baseline, propose the next shared checkpoint at a normal work boundary when five app-change batches have completed or 30 active app-work days have accumulated since the last checkpoint, whichever comes first. A multi-fix batch counts once; documentation-only work and inactive days do not count. Several releases within one batch do not create extra checkpoints. Record active days in the compact tracking block during ordinary task closeout; inactivity creates no job. These are adopted trial limits, not observed optimal values or a new release gate. Review their usefulness after two actual checkpoints.

Escalate a concrete data-loss/security report or blocked primary flow immediately under existing rules. Changed persistence/restore/schema, native framework/permissions, recurring regression, credible relevant dependency advisory, or materially slower offline interaction should prompt a targeted risk review during the current work. Broaden to the shared checkpoint only when the finding crosses flows or exposes a wider gap. Ordinary release checks and current specialist/artifact gates continue; a due checkpoint alone never requires another release audit or delays publication.

## One Shared Checkpoint

1. Lead/routine reads the current snapshot, owning revisions, relevant completed tasks and release/check evidence. Identify changed areas and reusable evidence before requesting work. Reuse named checks only under the CI policy's exact-source/input conditions; label older unchanged-area evidence with its source and limits. CI success is not native, accessibility or artifact evidence.
2. QA chooses a bounded cross-route sample: Planner week/day notes, schedule/templates and local saves; Ideas capture/edit/search/branch; Timer pause/resume/deadline; Finance visibility/session/records; Summaries and exports; Settings and JSON Merge/Replace recovery. Use synthetic data, offline operation, EN/FA/RTL, relevant short/portrait/landscape viewports, keyboard and accessible names/focus. Include critical save/navigation and backup recovery transitions across routes. Record pass/fail/unexecuted per selected scenario, not “whole app passed” from a sample.
3. On an available actual Android device, prioritize install/upgrade with retained synthetic records, cold start, suspension/relaunch, keyboard/rotation, file delivery, opted-in notification handling and TalkBack for changed or overdue risk areas. Record OS/device/APK version and scenario. Desktop/studio simulations remain explicitly separate. No device means unexecuted native coverage, with a follow-up owner; do not imply a phone or screen-reader test occurred. Existing release follow-up rules remain authoritative.
4. Product/Designer and only relevant specialists examine observed friction, failures and repeated effort. They may return no proposal. Return **at most three total proposals**, ranked by evidence and expected user value; each states problem, source/revision, value, uncertainty (`Needs confirmation` where unsupported), rough small/medium/large effort, risk, bounded scope, non-goals and acceptance signal. Avoid quotas and repeating prior requests without new evidence. Product owns scope; Designer receives the problem and constraints without prescribed layout. Consult `personal_systems` when a proposal connects Planner, Timer, Finance or Ideas into a personal routine.
5. Fold necessary maintenance, dependency/security signals and documentation drift into this same pass only when relevant. Owning implementation specialists identify concrete defects, recurring complexity or change-blocking duplication, bounded files and behavior-preservation checks. No speculative rewrites, backend restart or automatic dependency upgrades. Security handles credible exposure/advisory findings independently where required.

### Bounded Offline Performance Sample

Future execution uses a disposable synthetic baseline and a larger fixture: baseline 4 populated weeks/20 Ideas; larger 52 weeks with all ten section slots, 10 schedule entries per day and 500 Ideas of roughly 1 KB each, mixing EN/FA text. These sizes are trial stress cases, **Needs confirmation** as representative personal usage; they are not measured storage limits or promised targets. Do not use maintainer/private records or add performance instrumentation to the shipped app for this checkpoint.

QA records fixture seed/shape, source or APK revision, device/OS/WebView or desktop browser, offline state and measurement method. On the same environment, repeat three runs of cold startup, a week/route switch, opening/searching Ideas, a short typing-and-save interaction and reopen after save. Record observed delays/typing stalls/save completion and the three timings where a repeatable stopwatch or existing development tool is practical. Compare with the smaller fixture or previous comparable baseline; do not compare unlike devices/builds as a trend. Investigate reproducible input stalls, lost/delayed saves or a clear repeated slowdown; Product and QA decide whether it matters to the flow before prescribing a numeric performance target. No benchmark framework, broad load matrix or claim of executed performance QA is part of this policy definition.

## Discovery Sources And Skill Freshness

Use the remaining checkpoint budget for one relevant comparable-app/technology question or one skill gap, not a separate unsolicited study for every discipline. If there is no concrete question or need, skip research.

- Comparable-app work uses current dated primary sources: official app documentation, release notes, platform documentation or technical project releases. Start with at most two relevant planning/notes/adjacent apps and three sources. Cite URL, access date and release/publication date when available. Separate documented capability, vendor marketing claim, directly observed behavior and inference. Return at most two implications for Smart Paper with offline/privacy compatibility, expected value, feasibility uncertainty and rough effort; feed them into the shared three-proposal limit. A comparable capability does not justify copying it or expanding scope.
- Skill review starts with the existing local skill/version and a demonstrated responsibility gap. Compare at most two candidates/updates; record official publisher/repository, version/commit/date, instructions/provenance, required permissions/network/secrets, compatibility, expected benefit and maintenance burden. Recommend adopt/update/skip; missing provenance or unexplained access is a reason to defer. Never execute downloaded instructions, install/update/replace a skill or grant access just because it is new. Use the existing installation/plugin permission workflow only after an explicit scoped decision. No installation is authorized here.
- Product frames evidence using [smart-paper-product](../skills/smart-paper-product/SKILL.md). Relevant specialist feasibility review precedes prioritization; primary-source research is a bounded method for later execution, not research claimed in this policy task.

## Owners, Budget And Stop Conditions

Lead owns the checkpoint, acceptance and next action. Use `routine` routing for evidence inventory, due tracking and factual summaries; QA/tester for selected confidence checks; `product_design` for Product/Designer and applicable personal-systems consultation; `complex_implementation` for ambiguous feasibility/necessary maintenance; `critical_review` for independent high-risk correctness/security review. Resolve current models/effort from [.codex/model-routing.toml](../.codex/model-routing.toml); do not embed model IDs in policy. Invite only specialists needed by the evidence and reuse available agents.

Adopted trial budget: 30 minutes of active review work, at most two delegated bounded workstreams, one shared record and three proposals. Review budget is a cap, not a time estimate or reason to truncate required gates in an implementation task. No unrequested full builds/audits, multi-app research sweep or backend checks merely because the checkpoint is due. Stop when the selected evidence/scenarios and ranked findings are recorded, the cap is reached, there is no substantiated improvement, or unavailable devices/sources block further useful coverage. Mark remaining work unexecuted/deferred; do not repeat broad attempts. Urgent findings become separately scoped work under existing rules.

Manual read-only review and evidence recording are authorized by the adopted checkpoint policy within existing boundaries. A narrow reproduced fix already inside the agreed batch may follow existing design/test/review/release rules; this policy grants no extra implementation authority. New features, scope growth, uncertain behavior changes, skill/access changes and unresolved high-risk findings return to lead/maintainer prioritization. Proposals are not implementation approval.

The routine closeout owner updates the existing block rather than appending recurring reports or creating new chats. The baseline records exact revisions and resets the count for the next cue.

Success signals for the trial: a session can recognize due work from that block; selected QA has explicit evidence and native limits; any proposal traces to evidence and a bounded next decision; repeated evidence is reused correctly; the checkpoint stays within its cap and adds no release gate. After two checkpoints, lead/maintainer may keep, revise or discontinue the cues using recorded useful findings and effort. No telemetry or guaranteed token/defect reduction target.
